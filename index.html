🛵 How to Order

Ordering your favourite meal from QuickBite is simple and convenient. Follow these easy steps:

  1. Browse the Menu – Explore our burgers, pizzas, chicken meals, snacks, desserts and drinks.
  2. Choose Your Meal – Select your favourite food and click "Add to Cart".
  3. Review Your Cart – Check your selected meals, quantities and total order cost.
  4. Enter Delivery Details – Provide your name, phone number and delivery address.
  5. Select a Payment Method – Pay securely using M-Pesa, debit card, credit card or cash on delivery.
  6. Confirm Your Order – Review your order and click "Place Order".
  7. Enjoy Your Meal! – Our restaurant prepares your food and our delivery team brings it straight to your doorstep.
